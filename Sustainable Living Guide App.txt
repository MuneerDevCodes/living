Problem Statement

1. Introduction
1.1 Background and Necessity for the Mobile App
As awareness of climate change, environmental degradation, and resource depletion increases, many people are looking for ways to live more sustainably. However, the complexity of eco-friendly living—ranging from reducing waste and conserving energy to making ethical purchasing decisions—can overwhelm individuals who want to make a difference. While there is a wealth of information available on sustainable living, people often struggle to know where to begin or how to integrate these practices into their everyday routines.

In response to this need, there is a growing demand for tools that simplify sustainable living, helping individuals track their environmental impact and make informed, responsible choices. An app that educates users on sustainable practices, suggests eco-friendly products, and tracks their carbon footprint can empower users to make meaningful changes, while offering a personalized roadmap for adopting a greener lifestyle.

By providing the tools, information, and resources to make sustainable living easier and more accessible, the Sustainable Living Guide app will empower users to reduce their environmental impact and make more eco-conscious choices. Through tracking, education, and personalized recommendations, the app will help individuals live in a way that aligns with their values and contributes positively to the planet’s future.
1.2 Proposed Solution
The Sustainable Living Guide app will serve as an all-in-one platform to educate users on sustainable living, provide eco-friendly product recommendations, and offer carbon footprint tracking tools. The app will guide users through various aspects of their daily lives, from sustainable shopping to waste reduction, energy conservation, and conscious transportation choices. By tracking and providing actionable insights, the app will help users measure their impact and make positive changes that contribute to a more sustainable future.
1.3 Purpose of the Document
This document outlines the functional and non-functional requirements, technical specifications, and constraints of the Sustainable Living Guide App. It serves as a guideline for developers, designers, and stakeholders involved in the project.
1.4 Constraints
The development of the Sustainable Living Guide App must adhere to several constraints to ensure its successful implementation and operation. You may encounter constraints related to data storage, data synchronization, and backup procedures. This mobile app will be displaying various images and information, hence loading time should be minimized and fetching of data within the app should be fast and seamless. The usage of various images and videos may be subject to licensing agreements and copyright restrictions. It is important to understand and comply with these constraints to avoid legal issues
 
2. Functional Requirements
The app will be designed as a set of forms/pages, Navigation, and Fragments/Screens with menus representing choice of activities to be performed. 
Following are the functional requirements of the mobile app:

1.	Carbon Footprint Tracker: Users can input daily activities (e.g., transportation, energy usage, food consumption) to calculate their carbon footprint. The app will provide insights into the user’s carbon impact and offer recommendations to reduce it.
2.	Eco-Friendly Product Suggestions: Based on the user’s preferences and lifestyle, the app will suggest sustainable, eco-friendly products. This could include items like reusable water bottles, organic food options, energy-efficient appliances, and zero-waste alternatives.
3.	Sustainable Living Challenges: The app will feature weekly or monthly challenges to encourage users to adopt specific sustainable habits (e.g., a "Plastic-Free Week"). 
4.	Green Certifications & Labels Guide: The app will educate users on certifications and eco-labels (e.g., Fair Trade, Energy Star, Organic) to help them identify genuinely sustainable products and services when shopping.
5.	Waste Reduction Tracker: A feature to help users track their waste reduction efforts, including recycling habits, composting, and reducing single-use plastics. The app will provide tips and statistics to motivate users to improve their waste management.
6.	Sustainable Recipes & Meal Planning: The app will offer eco-friendly meal planning suggestions that focus on plant-based, locally sourced, and organic ingredients. This can help users reduce their food-related carbon footprint by making better food choices.
7.	Energy Conservation Tips: The app will provide users with personalized energy-saving tips for their home, such as turning off unused appliances, optimizing heating and cooling, or using renewable energy sources.
8.	Eco-Travel Suggestions: Recommendations for eco-friendly travel options, such as public transportation, carpooling, and sustainable hotels. The app will also offer sustainable travel tips to reduce the environmental impact of vacations and trips.
9.	Community Forum & Support: A platform where users can share their sustainability efforts, challenges, and success stories. This community aspect will foster a sense of support and inspiration for users as they embark on their eco-friendly journeys.
10.	Sustainability Progress Dashboard: A personalized dashboard that tracks a user’s sustainable living progress over time. Users can see improvements in areas like waste reduction, carbon footprint, and sustainable purchasing habits.
11.	Educational Content: Articles, videos, and infographics that provide users with information on environmental issues and sustainable practices. This content will help users stay informed and inspired to make positive changes.

4. Non-Functional Requirements
There are several non-functional requirements that should be fulfilled by the Mobile application.

These include:

Safe to use: The mobile app should not result in any malicious downloads or unnecessary file downloads.
Accessible: The mobile app should have clear and legible fonts, user-interface elements, and navigation elements.
User-friendly: The mobile app should be easy to navigate with clear menus and other elements and easy to understand.
Operability: The mobile app should operate in a reliably efficient manner.
Performance: The mobile app should demonstrate high value of performance through speed and throughput. In simple terms, the mobile app should be fast to load and page redirection should be smooth. 
Scalability: The system architecture and infrastructure should be designed to handle increasing user traffic, data storage, and feature expansions. 
Security: The mobile app should implement adequate security measures such as authentication. For example, only registered users can access certain features. 
Availability: The mobile app should be available 24/7 with minimum downtime.
