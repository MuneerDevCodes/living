# Sustainable Living Guide App – User Guide

## Introduction

Welcome to the **Sustainable Living Guide App**! This app empowers you to live more sustainably by tracking your carbon footprint, discovering eco-friendly products and recipes, participating in challenges, and connecting with a like-minded community.

---

## Getting Started

### 1. Installation
- **Android**: Download from the Google Play Store or install the APK provided by your administrator.
- **iOS**: Download from the App Store (if available).
- **Web**: Visit the provided web link.

### 2. Registration & Login
- Open the app and tap **Register** to create a new account.
- Fill in your details and submit.
- After registration, use your credentials to **Login**.
- If you forget your password, use the **Forgot Password** option.

---

## Main Features & How to Use Them

### 1. Home Page
- The **Home Page** is your dashboard, showing quick stats, recent activity, and navigation to all features.

### 2. Carbon Footprint
- Go to **Carbon Footprint** from the menu.
- Enter your daily/weekly activities (travel, energy use, etc.).
- View your carbon footprint summary and history.
- Use **Carbon Insights** for personalized tips to reduce your impact.

### 3. Challenges
- Visit the **Challenges** page to join eco-friendly challenges.
- Track your progress and earn rewards or badges.
- Compete with friends or the community.

### 4. Recipes
- Browse sustainable recipes in the **Recipes** section.
- Filter by category or ingredient.
- Save your favorites and add ingredients to your cart.

### 5. Eco Travel
- Explore eco-friendly travel tips and destinations.
- Log your trips to see their environmental impact.

### 6. Energy Tips
- Access daily or weekly energy-saving tips.
- Mark tips as completed to track your progress.

### 7. Waste Tracker
- Log your waste (plastic, food, etc.) in the **Waste Tracker**.
- Set goals to reduce waste and monitor your achievements.

### 8. Product Catalog & Cart
- Browse sustainable products in the **Product Catalog**.
- Add items to your **Cart** and place orders.
- Track your orders in the **Order Page**.

### 9. Forum
- Join discussions in the **Forum**.
- Post questions, share tips, and connect with the community.

### 10. Educational Content
- Read articles and guides in the **Educational Content** section.
- Stay informed about sustainability topics.

### 11. Certifications
- View and manage your eco-certifications.
- Learn how to earn new certifications.

### 12. Progress Dashboard
- Track your overall progress in sustainability.
- View achievements, badges, and completed challenges.

### 13. Profile & Settings
- Update your personal information in the **Profile** page.
- Adjust app preferences in **Settings**.

### 14. Contact Us & About
- Use **Contact Us** for support or feedback.
- Learn more about the app in the **About Us** section.

---

## Tips & Best Practices

- **Log Activities Regularly**: The more you log, the more accurate your insights.
- **Participate in Challenges**: Boost motivation and earn rewards.
- **Engage in the Forum**: Learn from others and share your journey.
- **Explore Educational Content**: Stay updated on sustainability trends.
- **Set Realistic Goals**: Use the dashboard to track and adjust your goals.

## FAQ & Troubleshooting

**Q: I can't log in. What should I do?**  
A: Use the "Forgot Password" option or contact support via the "Contact Us" page.

**Q: My data isn't saving.**  
A: Ensure you have a stable internet connection. Try restarting the app.

**Q: How do I reset my progress?**  
A: Go to **Settings** and look for the reset option, or contact support.

**Q: How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?**  
A: Use the **Contact Us** page to send feedback.
